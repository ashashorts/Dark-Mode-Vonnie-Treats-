About Vonnie Treats

The origin of Vonnie Treats started in the seventh grade with a heart‑shaped pan passed down from my grandma, along with her chocolate cake recipe. That pan and that recipe was the start of a new beginning. Seeing the reactions to that chocolate cake lit a fire that has yet to burn out. That moment showed me how powerful baking could be, and it became the foundation of my passion.

I created this site to build a space where people can come to see my baked goods, explore what I offer, and potentially purchase treats. Vonnie Treats is meant to be a welcoming place where customers can browse my creations, learn more about my baking journey, and stay connected as my business grows.

My motivation for Vonnie Treats is to bring a taste of home to my customers while connecting with different individuals. Every dessert I make carries a piece of my family, my memories, and the joy baking brings me. Through this business, I hope to share comfort, creativity, and warmth with everyone who visits or chooses to enjoy one of my treats.
